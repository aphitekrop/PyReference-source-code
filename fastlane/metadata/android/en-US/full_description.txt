PyReference is a personal Python reference that lives on your phone and works completely offline.

<b>400+ reference cards:</b>
<ul>
<li>keywords and operators</li>
<li>built-in functions</li>
<li>data types and their methods</li>
<li>exceptions</li>
<li>standard library modules</li>
<li>terminal commands and git</li>
<li>popular libraries</li>
</ul>

<b>Every card includes</b> syntax, parameters, return value, possible errors, examples and a "what to use instead" block.

<b>Useful articles:</b> step-by-step guides — from virtual environments to a Telegram bot and databases.

<b>More features:</b>
<ul>
<li>full-text search across all fields</li>
<li>cards sorted by popularity</li>
<li>favorites synced between phone and PC</li>
<li>100% offline</li>
</ul>

Free and open source.
